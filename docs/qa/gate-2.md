## 4. Conformance (code reading) and usability

What matches the design, all as screens.md and components.md describe:
- **Plant packing card (16B)** has three big buttons, `Packing started` → `Packed – how many bags?` → `Loaded`. They use `size="lg"` (59.5 px), with `text-2xl/3xl` buyer and bag numbers. A done step becomes a grey tick line and the future step is a disabled grey button. `Packing started` is disabled with `Wait for advance` while the advance is waiting. There is no money, no papers and no timeline. The Bags sheet prefills the planned bags and has −10/+10 buttons.
- **Dispatch form (17)** has a Truck / Container toggle (default from the buyer's country) and Port chips (default from the price basis). The Plant short form shows mode, number, port and bags, with the rest under More. It ends with a look-before-you-leap sheet that shows the next INV / SDN numbers, then `fn_order_dispatch`.
- **Checklist (18)** shows all 8 keys. Plant may tick only stuffing / COA, Untick is for owner and accounts only, and the BL number is required. A truck dispatch redirects back with `msg=no_papers`.
- **Money list (21)** is built from `v_order_balances`, with Late / Waiting / Paid pills, a WhatsApp Remind (T6) button and a totals line with "about ₹". **Money received (22)** has an order picker, `In rupees` with an editable rate for USD, `Bank` preselected and an over-payment confirm. Its success screen shows the REC number and `Share receipt` (T8).
- **Money pill rule:** buyers with nothing due show ₹0 / `Paid`, and the worst row wins.

## 4. Usability notes (plant supervisor with basic schooling, on a phone)

- Login is one screen: big "Plant" button (147×72, icon + one word) signs in with one tap; the only green button is Sign in; fields are 51 px; the eye toggle is 48 px and labelled. Good.
- The error when the server is unreachable is one plain sentence in a red bar ("No internet. Try again." / "ઇન્ટરનેટ નથી. ફરી કરો."); the button comes back to "Sign in" so a second try is obvious. Good.
- Empty email/password shows the browser's own English bubble, not the app's words (F6) — a Gujarati user sees English.
- Tapping the word "Password" does not open the keyboard (F2); users will tap the box, which works, but the label area is dead.
- Language toggle: the switch is instant on the same screen, no reload, Gujarati words render in Noto Sans Gujarati; the segments are a little short to hit (41 px, F3).
- Home (from code): a supervisor sees one tile ("Orders in production"), a "Packing today" list of cards (buyer — city, product line, bags × kg, ship by, status word) and "To do today" rows with round Call / WhatsApp buttons and a "Done" button; empty text "Nothing to do today. Well done." / "No order to pack today." Plain and short. Good.
- Home greeting uses the first name only ("Namaste, Dineshbhai"); the date is spelled `13 Nov 2026`. Good.
- Bottom bar: 5 slots, 24 px icons, 12.75 px words (the one allowed small size); More sheet rows are 59 px with 17 px words and show "Language" + "Sign out" at the bottom. Good.
- Header: the speaker (help) button is greyed and does nothing (spec stub). A supervisor may tap it repeatedly; consider hiding it until the clips exist, or a toast "Coming soon" in both languages.
- Staff (owner only): rows 72 px, sheet with 4 big job buttons and On/Off chips; module tick rows have a plain second line ("Today's work", "Buyers asking for price"). The own-row note "You cannot change your own job." is clear. Error toasts are English (F1).
- My Company: 7 fields + 3 bank fields visible, the rest under "More (export)"; GST and IFSC auto-uppercase; PIN opens the number keypad; Save is sticky above the bar. A 5-digit PIN gets the generic "Please fill this" rather than "PIN has 6 digits" — consider a specific message.
- On phones Staff / My Company have no back arrow (F5); the bottom bar is still there, so the user is not stuck.
- Bottom-of-page safe area and `pb-20` / `pb-28` padding are in place; nothing hides under the bar on the pages reviewed.

# Domain Research: Onion Dehydration Plant CRM (Mahuva, Gujarat)

Produced by the domain research agent on 2026-11-06. Sources are listed at the end.

## 1. Industry facts

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Mahuva (Bhavnagar district) is India's dehydrated onion capital: of ~160 dehydration plants in the Saurashtra region, nearly 120 are in and around Mahuva; the local industry consumes ~98% of the white onion crop harvested in the region and sells mainly to export. | https://commodity-board.com/?p=25215 |
| India holds roughly 70% of the world dehydrated onion market and about 90% of Indian dehydrated onion/garlic output is exported; monthly export volume rose from ~6,000 t to ~8,000 t in the latest reported season. | https://indiaseatradenews.com/record-breaking-export-of-dehydrated-onions-from-guj/ |
| Gujarat shipped 83,452 tonnes of dehydrated white onion in 2023-24 (APEDA figures quoted by a trade-data blog); India's HS 0712 (dried vegetables) exports were USD 283.1M in 2024 and USD 325.4M in 2025 (DGCI&S), of which 'dried onion pieces/powder' is USD 242.6M. | https://deepbeez.com/trade-flow/export-of-dehydrated-vegetables-from-india |
| Processing season: white onion harvest in Bhavnagar/Amreli lands Jan-Apr; dehydration generally runs February to July (some years start December or slip to April when raw onion is too costly). The plant is largely idle Aug-Jan, so CRM load is highly seasonal. | https://www.business-standard.com/article/markets/dehydration-units-doing-brisk-business-114022601315_1.html |
| Yield: about 8-10 kg of fresh white onion gives 1 kg of dehydrated product (Tridge May-2026 analysis says ~8:1; 2015 industry report says ~10:1). Raw material is 30-45% of final cost, dehydration energy 18-30%. | https://blog.tridge.com/blog-posts/how-dried-onion-flakes-really-moveand-where-your-cost-quality-and-risk-get-locked-in |
| Viability hinges on raw onion price: industry is profitable when fresh white onion is Rs 3-7/kg and unviable above ~Rs 10/kg (All India Dehydration Association president Vitthal Koradiya, 2015). Mahuva APMC white onion was Rs 100-145 per 20 kg (Rs 5-7/kg) in May 2022. | https://www.freshplaza.com/north-america/article/2142280/india-dehydrated-onion-exports-down-70/ |
| Export price benchmarks: Indian dried onion flake export prices ranged USD 1.88-2.92/kg in 2024 (Tridge); supplier listings quote USD 1,800-2,400/MT FOB/CIF for white/red flakes-kibbled; white onion powder FOB India ~EUR 1.43/kg (commodity-board, 2026). Domestic listing: white onion kibbled Rs 185/kg (TradeIndia, 100 kg MOQ). | https://dir.tridge.com/prices/dried-onion-flakes/IN |
| Russia and Ukraine took ~18,000 t/yr (30% of Mahuva's dehydrated onion exports) before the 2022 war; exports stopped because buyer payments became uncertain and payment cycles lengthened (All India Vegetables Dehydration Association, May 2022). Other key markets: USA, Germany, UK, Netherlands, France, UAE, Saudi Arabia. | https://krishijagran.com/agriculture-world/why-the-export-of-white-onion-is-constantly-declining/ |
| Ports: Pipavav (APM Terminals) is ~27 km from a Mahuva plant; Mundra is also used (trade records show dehydrated onion from Mundra to Gdynia, Poland in 22 kg cartons). Container load for kibbled/flakes is typically 12-15 MT per 20 ft (listings: 12 MT, 13.5 MT, 14-15 MT); powder is denser (up to 18-20 MT). | https://nutrada.com/suppliers/chhatariya-foods-pvt-ltd |
| Packaging norms: 20-25 kg multiwall paper bags with PE liner, 25 kg HDPE/PP bags with food-grade liner, or two poly bags inside one corrugated (CFB) box of 20/22/24/25 kg net. Moisture spec: powder <6%; flakes commonly 8-10% or lower (Indian exporters usually spec 5-6%). Mesh: powder 80-100 mesh, granules 40-60 mesh; kibbled 3-5 mm, chopped 1-3 mm, minced 0.5-1 mm. | https://geminate-international.pages.dev/blog/complete-buyer-guide-sourcing-bulk-dried-vegetables-india |
| Lead times quoted by Indian exporters: production and packing 2-4 weeks, sea freight 3-6 weeks, total order-to-delivery 6-10 weeks; MOQ 500 kg-1 MT per product; sample plus COA requested before every first container. | https://geminate-international.pages.dev/blog/complete-buyer-guide-sourcing-bulk-dried-vegetables-india |
| Payment terms seen in the trade: 20-30% advance by TT against proforma invoice and balance against BL copy (one Indian listing: 20% PI advance, 80% against BL copy); LC at sight for new buyers; buyers requesting 30-day credit against BL copy; Freshdi data (Sep 2026) shows L/C ~30% of dehydrated white onion deals. | https://sourcing.hktdc.com/en/Product-Detail/Dehydrated-Onion-1X1X9GJ5 |
| Export registrations: IEC from DGFT; APEDA RCMC (dehydrated vegetables are an APEDA scheduled product) costs Rs 5,900 (Rs 5,000 + 18% GST), valid 5 years; FSSAI Central Licence is mandatory for exporters (~Rs 7,500/yr, one per premises); phytosanitary certificate via the PQMS portal (pqms.cgg.gov.in, one-time exporter registration then per-consignment application). | https://apeda.gov.in/sites/default/files/documents/2026-06/Registration_Procedure.pdf |
| Since 1 Jan 2025 non-preferential Certificates of Origin are issued only electronically through DGFT's eCoO 2.0 (trade.gov.in) with a chamber of commerce as issuing agency; chambers charge roughly Rs 200-500 per certificate and need scanned invoice + packing list. | https://kanarachamber.com/certificate-of-origin |
| Domestic tax mechanics: dried onion is HSN 07122000; GST rate is 5% per Cleartax/Schedule I (some aggregator sites show nil - must be confirmed with the client's CA); e-way bill needed for consignments over Rs 50,000; e-invoicing mandatory above Rs 5 crore turnover; Section 43B(h) (from AY 2024-25) pushes buyers to pay MSME suppliers within 45 days. | https://busy.in/hsn/hsn-07122000.md |
| Inquiry channels: IndiaMART allocates BuyLeads weekly (Verified Exporter Silver = 10 foreign leads/week, Gold = 25; unused leads lapse Saturday 11:59 PM); seller pages carry Call and WhatsApp buttons. Mahuva firms (Parth Foods, Ganga Foods, Sky Agri Export, Century Foods) exhibit at Gulfood Dubai; APEDA India Pavilion had 161 exhibitors at Gulfood 2026. | https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/queries-indiamart-verified-exporter |
| Plant staffing evidence: a Mahuva dehydration plant lists 26-50 employees; Mahuva job ads ask for an Accountant with Tally ERP (sales, purchase, receipt entries), an Export Documentation executive (invoice, packing list, BL, LC documents), a Sales Executive to find domestic and international buyers, and an Accounts Executive at Maharaja Dehydration. | https://www.expertia.ai/kazmainfoods/job/hiring-accountant-6821963ab438bfaef8682e71 |
| Literacy and language: Bhavnagar district literacy 75.5% (male 84.4%, female 66.1%); Mahuva town 51.9% and Mahuva-taluka villages 43-67% per census aggregators (Census 2011); Gujarati is spoken by 97.9% of the district. | https://www.censusindia2011.com/gujarat/bhavnagar/mahuva/mahuva-m-population.html |
| Tools used today: Tally is used by one in four Indian MSMEs (7M+ customers, FY24 revenue Rs 625 crore, Mint 2025); WhatsApp Business had 15M+ active business accounts in India by end-2024; 57% of India's active internet users are rural and 98% access the internet in Indic languages (IAMAI-Kantar); Android held 89.6% of Indian smartphone shipments in Q3 2025 (IDC). | https://yourstory.com/smbstory/tallys-nupur-goenka-charts-a-new-digital-course-for-msmes |

## 2. Business process (how a plant sells today)

| Stage | Who | Tool today | Pain | CRM must do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Inquiry arrives (Puchhparchh) | Sales person (export leads); owner (domestic brokers and old buyers) | IndiaMART Lead Manager app, WhatsApp, phone call, visiting cards in a drawer, Excel on one laptop | Leads scattered across IndiaMART, WhatsApp and the owner's head; no one knows which inquiries were never answered; IndiaMART leads expire if not consumed. | One big 'New Inquiry' button: buyer name, country/city, phone (WhatsApp), product, quantity, source. Auto-code INQ-xxxx. Show today's unanswered inquiries as a red count on the home screen. |
| 2. Qualify and reply | Sales person; owner approves the rate verbally | WhatsApp, a PDF product brochure, Excel price sheet | Rate given from memory; different rate given to two buyers; owner's floor price not visible to sales; no record of what was promised. | Inquiry card with status chips (New / Replied / Sample / Quoted / Won / Lost). Price list with owner-set floor price so sales cannot quote below it. 'Send on WhatsApp' button that opens wa.me with a pre-filled text. |
| 3. Sample dispatch with COA | Sales person raises; plant supervisor packs; munim books courier and pays | Courier AWB slip, WhatsApp photo of the sample packet, notebook | Nobody follows up after 2 weeks; AWB numbers lost; buyer asks 'which sample lot was that?' and no one knows. | Sample record (SMP-xxxx) linked to inquiry: product, lot, grams, courier + AWB, date sent, feedback (OK / Not OK / Waiting). Auto follow-up reminder after 7 and 14 days. |
| 4. Quotation | Sales person drafts; owner approves (floor price, terms) | Word/Excel template on laptop, exported to PDF, sent on WhatsApp/email | Typos in USD amounts, wrong incoterm, quotes not numbered, cannot find last quote to the same buyer, revisions overwrite the original. | Quote in 5 fields (product, qty, unit price, incoterm, payment terms); auto number QTN/26-27/0012; PDF with company letterhead, GSTIN/IEC; one-tap WhatsApp share; owner approval required if price < floor; status Sent / Accepted / Rejected / Expired. |
| 5. Buyer confirms - Proforma Invoice and advance | Sales creates PI; munim checks bank credit; owner confirms | Excel PI, bank SMS/net banking, Tally receipt entry | Advance arrives but nobody tells sales; or production starts before advance; PI and quote differ; FX rate on receipt not recorded. | One click 'Convert quote to Order' makes ORD-xxxx and PI PDF. Payment screen: 'Advance received?' with amount, date, USD and INR. Order shows a green 'Advance OK' badge before production can be marked started. |
| 6. Production and packing plan | Plant supervisor; owner decides raw onion purchase | Notebook, whiteboard, phone calls, lab COA on paper | Supervisor does not know the exact packing spec promised to the buyer (bag print, net weight, cut size); last-minute changes; owner asks 'how much is packed for Dubai?' and gets a guess. | Order has a plain 'Packing card' the supervisor can read on phone: product, cut, moisture, bag type, bags count, label text, ship-by date. Supervisor taps 'Packing started' / 'Packed: 480 bags' / 'COA uploaded (photo)'. No other data entry asked from him. |
| 7. Pre-shipment booking and certificates | Munim/export documentation person with the CHA; owner signs | CHA on WhatsApp/email, PQMS website, chamber website, paper files | Documents sit with CHA; owner cannot see what is pending; missed cut-off means container rolls to next vessel and buyer complains. | Shipment checklist with 8 tick boxes (container booked, stuffing date, phyto, CoO, fumigation, COA, invoice+packing list, BL received) with date and photo/PDF upload. Home screen shows 'Documents pending: 3' in orange. |
| 8. Dispatch and shipping | Munim + CHA; sales informs buyer | Email/WhatsApp PDFs, courier AWB, Tally sales invoice | Buyer asks for docs repeatedly; originals couriered before money arrives; nobody records container number, vessel, ETA. | Dispatch record: container no., truck no., stuffing date, port, vessel, ETD/ETA, BL no., docs sent date. Generate commercial invoice + packing list PDF from the order. 'Share docs on WhatsApp' bundle. |
| 9. Balance payment and bank closure | Munim; owner chases buyer on WhatsApp | Bank portal, Tally, notebook of 'baaki' (outstanding) | Owner does not know total outstanding across buyers in one place; overdue amounts found only when the munim is asked; original BL released by mistake. | Simple 'Baaki' (outstanding) screen: per buyer, amount due, days overdue in red; record receipt in 3 taps (amount, date, USD/INR). Never a double-entry ledger - Tally stays the book of account. |
| 10. Domestic sale (masala / snack / noodle makers) | Owner takes order; munim bills in Tally; supervisor loads truck | Tally Prime (invoice, e-way bill), phone, WhatsApp | Orders taken verbally; rate disputes at payment time; credit limit exceeded for slow payers; e-way bill details re-typed. | Domestic order in Rs/kg with GST auto-calc and credit days; 'Order confirmation' PDF on WhatsApp; outstanding and credit-days alert per buyer. Export CSV/Tally XML so the munim does not re-type invoices. |
| 11. Follow-up and repeat business | Sales person; owner for key accounts | WhatsApp broadcast lists, memory | Old buyers forgotten; no list of who bought what last season at what price; no reason recorded for lost quotes. | Buyer page shows last 5 orders with price and date; 'Follow up on' date on every inquiry/quote with a daily 'Today's follow-ups' list; lost reason as 4 chips (Price / Quality / Timing / No reply). |

## 5. Simple UI principles (for users with basic schooling)

| Principle | Why | Example | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| One job per screen, max 6 fields, big primary button at the bottom | Lower-literacy users read word by word, cannot skim, and lose concentration when scrolling; Nielsen's rewrite raised lower-literacy task success from 46% to 82%. | 'New Inquiry' = buyer name, phone, country, product (chips), quantity (number + MT/kg toggle), source (chips). One green 'Save' button. No tabs, no accordion. | https://www.nngroup.com/articles/writing-for-lower-literacy-users/ |
| Numbers can stay, words must shrink: show quantities, rates and dates as big digits; use icons plus a one-word Gujarati/Hindi label | Medhi, Sagar and Toyama found semi-literate users easily recognise numerals even when they cannot read, and preferred semi-abstract cartoons to abstract icons; text-only UI had 0% task completion vs up to 100% for text-free versions. | Order card: '12,000 kg' and '$1.95/kg' in 24px, a bag icon with 'Packing' under it, a ship icon with 'Dispatch'. Never 'Opportunity', 'SKU' or 'Fulfilment'. | https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/mas-965-nextlab-i-designing-mobile-technologies-for-the-next-billion-users-fall-2008/8ccecb3eacd514b7ea0255f5158de907_MITMAS_965F08_medhi2007.pdf |
| Status as colour chips the owner already uses: red = money/attention, orange = waiting, green = done | Khata apps (OkCredit, Khatabook) succeeded by reducing bookkeeping to one number per customer with a clear direction; colour carries meaning without reading. | Buyer list row: 'Al-Barakah  $16,380  32 days' in red; 'Everest  Rs 0' in green. Inquiry chips: New (blue), Replied (orange), Quoted (purple), Won (green), Lost (grey). | https://okcredit.in/udhari-app |
| No accounting vocabulary; name things the way the munim and seth say them | Vyapar removed the 'credit' and 'debit' sections because most MSME owners do not know accounting terms; OkCredit's test was that shopkeepers could use it without a demo. | Use 'Baaki' / 'Outstanding', 'Paisa aavya' / 'Money received', 'Bhaav' / 'Rate', 'Puchhparchh' / 'Inquiry'. Never 'Receivable', 'Debit note', 'Pipeline stage'. | https://yourstory.com/smbstory/msme-accounting-software-gst-bengaluru-vyapar/amp |
| Gujarati and Hindi first, English as fallback, with a language switch on the login screen | 98% of Indian internet users access content in Indic languages; Gujarati is spoken by 97.9% of Bhavnagar district; Google NBU research says a single hard-coded language quietly excludes shared-device households. | labels.ts holds gu/hi/en strings for all 13 module keys; product and buyer names stay as typed; numbers formatted 12,00,000 (lakh style) with Rs and $ symbols. | https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/01/16/dcm51-biz-iamai-internet-usage-report.html |
| Phone-first layout, thumb-reachable, 48px+ tap targets, works on a cracked 5.5-inch Android with slow 4G | Android is ~90% of Indian smartphones; the supervisor and owner only have phones; Google's Build-for-Billions guidance: support slow networks, low-end devices, small app size. | Home = 4 tiles stacked vertically; lists are cards not tables; forms open as full-screen pages, not modals; PDF opens in the phone's viewer; 'Save' still works when network drops (retry queue). | https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/build-for-billions |
| WhatsApp is the output channel: every document and reminder has a 'Send on WhatsApp' button, no email required | 15M+ Indian businesses run on WhatsApp Business; Khatabook/Vyapar grew by sending reminders and bills on WhatsApp; IndiaMART seller pages already expose WhatsApp buttons. | Quote saved -> PDF -> button opens wa.me/<buyer number>?text=<Gujarati/English message> with the PDF shared from the phone share sheet; payment reminder uses the same path. | https://vyaparapp.in/gst-billing-app |
| Prefer picking over typing: chips, dropdowns with pictures, phone contact picker, camera for documents | IDEO/Google NBU research: new users struggle with typing and fear 'clicking the wrong button'; Bikayi onboarded merchants with phone-number login and photo-based catalogues in 90 seconds. | Product = 6 chips with cut-size pictures (flakes/kibbled/chopped/minced/granules/powder); country = flag list; COA = 'Take photo'; buyer phone from contacts. | https://www.ideo.com/blog/5-tools-to-design-for-digital-confidence |
| Let users 'look before they leap': preview every PDF and show a confirmation summary before anything is sent or numbered | Digital-confidence research: users want previews and risk-free trials to avoid fearing mistakes; the munim fears spoiling records. | 'Create PI' shows a one-screen summary (buyer, 12,000 kg, $23,400, 30% advance $7,020) with 'Looks right - Create' and 'Change'. Documents are editable drafts until 'Send'. | https://www.ideo.com/blog/5-tools-to-design-for-digital-confidence |
| Guided help instead of manuals: a 20-second voice/video clip in Gujarati on each screen and an always-visible '?' help button | Medhi et al. identified a consistent help feature as a previously overlooked need; Google NBU recommends video tutorials for first-time users. | Each page header has a speaker icon that plays a Gujarati voice note: 'Aa screen ma navi puchhparchh lakho...'. Same position on every screen. | https://design.google/library/connectivity-culture-and-credit |
| Minimal data entry, smart defaults: pre-fill from the last order and from masters; never ask the same thing twice | OkCredit and Khatabook replaced paper ledgers only because an entry takes seconds; staff will abandon any screen that is slower than WhatsApp. | Quote for Al-Barakah pre-fills last season's product, packing, incoterm (CIF Jebel Ali) and terms (30/70); only quantity and rate are typed. | https://kr-asia.com/indian-mom-and-pop-stores-use-okcredit-to-digitize-sales-records-startup-stories |

Season totals are 3 numbers, one optional bar. | https://www.nngroup.com/articles/writing-for-lower-literacy-users/ |

## 6. Must-have features

| Priority | Feature | Plain label | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Inquiry capture with source, product chips, quantity, WhatsApp number; auto INQ code; status chips | Nava Grahak / New Inquiry (puchhparchh) | Every sale starts here; leads are currently lost across IndiaMART, WhatsApp and memory. |
| P0 | Buyer master (export/domestic type, country, incoterm, payment terms, credit days, GSTIN/IEC, contacts with WhatsApp) | Grahak / Buyers (parties) | Already in migration 0002; everything hangs off the buyer page; owner thinks in buyers, not deals. |

| P0 | Quotation builder (5 fields) with auto number QTN/FY, PDF on letterhead, WhatsApp share, validity, Accept/Reject/Expire | Bhaav Patra / Quotation | The quote is the main sales document; today it is a hand-edited Excel with errors. |

| P1 | Supervisor packing card and 3-button progress (Packing started / Packed with bag count / Loaded) plus COA photo upload | Packing card / Packing nu kaam | Connects plant floor to the order without asking a 10th-pass supervisor to type; gives owner live status. |
| P1 | Shipment checklist with 8 tick boxes (container booked, stuffing, phyto, CoO, fumigation, COA, invoice+PL, BL) with dates and file upload; container, vessel, BL no., ETA | Shipment ni checklist / Container documents | Export documentation is the munim's biggest stress; a checklist is simpler than a document-generation engine. |
| P1 | Commercial invoice and packing list PDF generated from the order (export) and GST invoice PDF (domestic, HSN 07122000, 5%) with FY-based doc numbers | Bill / Invoice banavo | Removes re-typing and number mismatches between quote, PI and invoice; doc_counters already exist. |
| P1 | WhatsApp share buttons (wa.me deep links with prefilled Gujarati/English text) on quote, PI, invoice, payment reminder, follow-up | WhatsApp par moklo / Send on WhatsApp | WhatsApp is the only channel buyers and staff actually use; no email dependency. |
| P1 | Gujarati / Hindi / English UI switch and lakh-crore number formatting; USD and INR shown together | Bhasha / Language | Primary users read Gujarati; export money is in USD but the owner thinks in Rs. |
| P1 | Export to Tally-friendly CSV/XML of invoices and receipts | Tally ma moklo / Send to Tally | Munim will not re-type; CRM must not pretend to replace Tally. |
| P2 | Season summary (3 numbers: MT sold, amount billed, outstanding) and lost-reason chips | Season no hisab / Season summary | Owner wants a season view; keep it to numbers, no chart gallery. |
| P2 | Voice/video help in Gujarati per screen and an in-app 'Call support' button | Madad / Help | Low-literacy users rely on a consistent help feature and person-to-person trust rather than manuals. |
| P2 | Audit trail readable as sentences ('Hardik changed rate 1.95 -> 1.90 on 12 Feb') | Kone shu badalyu / Who changed what | Owner trust: who changed a price or deleted a payment; audit_log already exists. |

## 7. Features to avoid

| Feature | Why avoid |
|---|---|
| Kanban pipeline with 7+ drag-and-drop stages, lead scoring, weighted forecasts | Owner thinks in 'inquiry, sample, quote, order, money'; drag-drop fails on phones; scoring is meaningless with 50-150 inquiries per season. |
| Double-entry accounting, GL, bank reconciliation, GST returns | Tally is used by 1 in 4 MSMEs and by the munim here; duplicating it creates two books of truth and kills adoption. CRM records receipts and outstanding only. |
| Full production/MRP, inventory valuation, raw-onion procurement module in v1 | Scope creep into ERP; supervisor will not log batches; keep a packing card and bag count only. |
| Custom field builder, workflow automation designer, email sequences, campaign module | Nobody in the plant will configure them; emails are rarely used by buyers from Mahuva plants; WhatsApp deep links cover 90% of messaging. |
| Dashboards with many charts, pivot reports, exports to BI | Lower-literacy users cannot parse multi-column, multi-chart pages; three numbers and a list answer the owner's questions. |
| English-only jargon (Opportunity, SKU, Fulfilment, AR, Pipeline, Lead Qualification Framework) | Users read Gujarati/Hindi and only know trade words like FOB, LC, container; jargon causes abandonment. |
| Desktop-only data tables with 12 columns and modal dialogs | Owner and supervisor use phones; tables overflow, modals trap users; cards and full-screen forms instead. |
| Mandatory fields everywhere, complex validation and multi-step wizards | Staff will enter dummy data or stop using the app; allow saving with name + phone and fill the rest later. |
| LC document compliance engine, UCP600 checking, customs filing integration in v1 | CHA and bank handle these; a checklist with uploads is enough for a small plant; build integrations only after adoption. |
| Multi-currency ledgers with live FX feeds and hedging | Owner needs 'USD amount and the Rs we got'; one manual FX rate on receipt is enough. |
| Per-user granular permission matrices with dozens of toggles | Four roles with module gating (already built) is what owners understand: owner sees cost, others do not. |
| Separate mobile app with login via email + password reset flows | Phone-number or simple credential login on a responsive web app; email is not used by these users (Bikayi pattern). |

## 10. Glossary (plain meanings)

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Dehydrated white onion (safed dungli / pyaaz) | White onion dried to ~5-6% moisture; mild, preferred by export buyers. Mahuva's main product. |
| Red / pink onion | Cheaper dried red (Nashik) or pink onion; stronger taste; used for domestic masala and some export. |
| Flakes | Biggest pieces, 8-20 mm slices; used in soups and pizza toppings. |
| Kibbled | Medium pieces 3-5 mm (sometimes 5-8 mm); most common export cut. |
| Chopped | Small pieces 1-3 mm. |
| Minced | Very fine pieces 0.5-1 mm. |
| Granules | Coarse powder, 40-60 mesh (also 8-16 or 16-26 mesh grades). |
| Powder | Fine powder, 80-100 mesh; moisture must be below 6% or it cakes. |
| Mesh | Sieve size; higher number = finer. 40 mesh is coarse, 100 mesh is fine. |
| Moisture % | Water left in the product; buyers want 5-6% (max 8-10%). Too wet = fungus and claims. |
| COA (Certificate of Analysis) | Lab report for one lot: moisture, mesh, colour, bacteria (E. coli absent/1 g, Salmonella absent/25 g). Goes with every sample and shipment. |
| Lot / batch number | Number printed on bags so a complaint can be traced to the day's production. |
| MT (metric tonne) / kg | 1 MT = 1,000 kg = 40 bags of 25 kg. Export prices are per kg or per MT in USD; domestic per kg in Rs. |
| 20 ft / 40 ft container (FCL) | One 20 ft box holds about 12-15 MT of kibbled/flakes (480-600 bags of 25 kg); powder can reach 18-20 MT. |
| 25 kg paper bag / HDPE bag / CFB carton | Standard packing: multiwall paper bag with plastic liner, or two poly bags inside a corrugated box of 20-25 kg. |
| FOB (Free On Board) Pipavav / Mundra | Our price includes delivery onto the ship at the Indian port; buyer pays sea freight and insurance. |
| CIF / CFR Jebel Ali | Our price includes sea freight (and insurance for CIF) to the buyer's port. |
| EXW / ex-plant | Buyer collects from our factory gate; common for domestic sales. |
| Proforma Invoice (PI) | Advance bill sent so the buyer can pay the advance or open an LC; not the final GST/commercial invoice. |
| TT advance / balance against BL copy | Buyer wires 20-30% before production and the rest when we show the scanned Bill of Lading. |
| LC at sight | Buyer's bank promises to pay when our bank presents correct documents; safer but paperwork must match exactly. |
| CAD / DP | Cash against documents: buyer pays their bank to get the original shipping documents. |
| Bill of Lading (BL) | Shipping line's receipt and title document for the container; original released only after balance payment. |
| Shipping bill | Customs export declaration filed by the CHA on ICEGATE; needed for e-BRC and GST refund. |
| CHA / freight forwarder | Agent who books the container, files customs and arranges the truck to port. |
| Phytosanitary certificate (Phyto) | Government plant-health certificate from PQMS portal; many countries demand it even for dried onion. |
| Certificate of Origin (CoO) | Chamber-issued proof that goods are made in India (eCoO 2.0 online since 2025). |
| Fumigation certificate | Proof the container/goods were treated against pests; demanded by some buyers/countries. |
| IEC / RCMC / FSSAI / GSTIN / AD code | The five registration numbers printed on export documents: import-export code, APEDA membership, food licence, GST number, bank authorised-dealer code. |
| e-BRC | Bank's electronic proof that export money was received; needed to close the shipping bill. |
| GST 5% / HSN 07122000 | Tax code and rate for dried onion on domestic invoices; exports are zero-rated (LUT). |
| e-way bill | Online transport slip required for any domestic truck load worth over Rs 50,000. |
| Credit days / Baaki (udhaar) | Days the domestic buyer may take to pay (15-45 days under MSME rule); 'baaki' is the outstanding amount. |
| Season | Feb-July processing window after the Jan-Apr white onion harvest; the plant is nearly closed Aug-Jan. |
| Floor price | Lowest rate the owner allows sales to quote; set from raw onion cost x yield (8-10 kg raw per kg dry). |

## 11. Risks

- GST rate ambiguity: third-party HSN sites show 0% and 5% for HSN 0712; Stage 0 seeds 5% (Cleartax/Schedule I). Confirm with the client's CA and make the rate editable per product, not hard-coded.
- Seasonality: plants run Feb-July; a demo in Nov-Jan must use last-season data and the pilot should launch before February or the first real usage is 8 months away.
- Adoption risk: if the munim must type invoices twice (CRM + Tally) he will stop; Tally export/import must be in P1 and demoed, or the CRM must clearly not try to be the billing system.
- Owner trust/privacy: cost prices and buyer lists are the plant's crown jewels; product_costs must stay owner/accountant-only (already in RLS) and the demo must show the lock, or the owner will refuse to let sales use it.
- WhatsApp dependence: wa.me deep links work on phones only and need the phone to have the PDF; WhatsApp Business API costs money and needs Meta approval - keep v1 on deep links and plan API later.
- Connectivity in GIDC/village plants is patchy; forms must tolerate network drops (retry, no data loss) or supervisors will not use the packing buttons.
- Low literacy is uneven: owners of larger plants are graduates, supervisors may be 8th pass; a single UI must be readable by both - test with real Mahuva staff, not assumptions (census aggregator literacy figures for Mahuva town vary 52-76%).
- Price volatility: raw onion swings (Rs 3-7/kg viable vs Rs 10+ unviable) mean floor prices change weekly; if floor price is hard to edit, sales will bypass it.
- Payment risk and FX: Russia/Ukraine 2022 shows buyers can stop paying; the CRM must make 'original BL released?' and 'balance due' impossible to miss, and FX must be captured manually per receipt.
- Regulatory churn: eCoO 2.0 (2025), PQMS replacing PQIS, 43B(h) 45-day rule, e-invoice thresholds change; keep documents as checklists and templates, not deep integrations, so changes are label edits.
- Scope creep into ERP/production: owners will ask for raw onion purchase, labour and yield tracking; park it in P2/backlog to protect simplicity.
- Evidence gaps: no published survey of Mahuva plant staff education or tools; role/tool evidence comes from job ads, census aggregators and app-usage statistics - validate with 3-5 plant visits before design freeze.
- Local dev environment: Postgres and Supabase are not running; design/dev agents must start them before touching migrations for leads/samples/quotes/orders/payments.
- Multi-tenant template: the same code must fit other Mahuva plants (garlic, red onion, powder-only); keep product forms, document templates and labels data-driven per organization.
